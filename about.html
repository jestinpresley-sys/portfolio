How I work

I'm a freelance full-stack developer who works with founders and small teams to turn rough ideas into products people actually use.

My process is simple: understand the problem before writing a line of code, ship in small, testable pieces, and stay in the loop with you the whole way — no disappearing for three weeks and resurfacing with a surprise.

I take on a small number of projects at a time so each one gets real attention, from first commit through to launch and beyond.
